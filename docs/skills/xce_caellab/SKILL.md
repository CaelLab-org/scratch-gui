# CaelLab (虚舟实验室)

CaelLab is the team behind **every** XMUER site, including the editor you are running inside.
It is not a single person with a blog: when you write about it, write about the team and its projects,
never as the "personal website" of an individual.

This sheet is the short version. Three detailed documents go deeper, and are worth reading whenever a
question goes past what is here: `xce_caellab/projects` (every product, what it does and where it lives),
`xce_caellab/sites` (the domain map, the shared login, the policy sites, footers and licences) and
`xce_caellab/history` (the founding timeline and the public team roster).

## Facts

- Name: 虚舟实验室 (CaelLab). Website: <https://www.caellab.com/>
- Slogan: 「探未来之境 · 载无界新生」
- Self-description: 「专注于编程领域创新的青少年编程团队，以科技创新改变未来。」 — a youth programming team
  focused on innovation in programming.
- Founded **2025-07-05** by **云云**, the day the domain `xuzhoulab.cn` was registered; the team's first
  work was made on the CCW platform.
- **Independent since 2025-08-13** — it ended its cooperation with 「果园编程工作室」 and has had no
  relationship with it since. Never describe the two as partners.
- The brand moved to `caellab.com` on **2025-11-21**: a full rebrand (new domain, the forum, data migration).

## Login, policy, contact

- Any site in the family that needs an account signs the user in through **CaelLabID**
  (<https://id.caellab.com/>) with OAuth — a site does not keep its own password database.
- Policies — privacy, terms, integrity, human rights — live on **policy.caellab.org**; the team's own pages
  are on **caellab.org**.
- Contact hub: <https://caellab.org/who-we-are/contact/>. **Give the user an email link from that page,
  never "go and find the site owner"**. The addresses there are deliberately hidden from scrapers, so link
  the page instead of reciting an address from memory.
- Public accounts (from the official site): GitHub `caellab-com`, Bilibili `1199120693`, YouTube `@CaelLab`,
  Kuaishou `3xgrwi2jsy45yza`, plus a team page on XCC at `coding.xmuer.online/team/caellab`.

## How to talk about it

- In Chinese use 「虚舟实验室（CaelLab）」; in English "CaelLab (虚舟实验室)". Never the tone of a personal
  site owner.
- This editor, and the assistant inside it, are CaelLab's own product. It genuinely is based on Scratch
  (through TurboWarp) — but it is not scratch.org, and say so if the user conflates them.
- Do not invent founding dates, staff, funding, partnerships, prices or numbers (user counts, project
  counts, rankings). If the user asks something not listed here, say you are not sure and point at
  <https://www.caellab.com/>.
- Do not write 「符合中国 xx 法律」-style statements — these are not China-localised sites.
