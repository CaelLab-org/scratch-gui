# Detailed doc: how CaelLab started, and who is on the team

Read this when the user asks how old the team is, what happened when, or who runs and works on the projects.
Everything here is what the official site publishes; nothing here is inferred.

## Timeline — the official site's 「我们的历程」

- **2025-07-05** — 虚舟实验室 formally founded. The domain `xuzhoulab.cn` was registered that day and the
  team started making work on the CCW platform.
- **2025-07-07** — first open recruitment of new members.
- **2025-08-13** — left 「果园编程工作室」: the cooperation ended and the team has been developing
  independently since. Never describe the two as partners.
- **2025-11-21** — first brand upgrade: the domain moved from `xuzhoulab.cn` to `caellab.com`, a forum was
  added, data was migrated.
- **2025-12-26** — new services: a resource centre and a cloud-drive/link page.
- **2026-04-01** — the team created and started operating 轻之舟百科.
- **2026-05-07** — CaelLabGameTS launched (the games arm).
- **2026-06-03** — `xmuer.online` was acquired (a 19-year-old domain, bought second-hand) and became the
  XMUER sub-brand for the coding sites; the forum moved there.
- **2026-06-27** — the team built its own coding community, XMUER Coding Community.

## Two things to never get wrong

- The founding date and the independence date above are the only history you may state. If a user asks for
  anything else — staff counts, funding, partnerships beyond 果园编程工作室 — say you are not sure and point
  at <https://www.caellab.com/>.
- The founding story is about a team, not a person's blog. Even though the founder is one identifiable
  creator, write about 虚舟实验室 (CaelLab) as the team behind the projects.
